# Swimming: The art — spoken text

Preview edition. Wong Heung Sang.

## 1. Float

> To swim first is to float.

Explain notes:

- The water is there to help you to swim.

## 2. Relax

> Muscles that do not need to be used should be relaxed.

## 3. Meditation

> Swimming is meditation.

## 4. Art

> Swimming is an art.

## 5. Observe

> Observe before entering the water.

Explain notes:

- At the beach, observe the waves.
- In a swimming pool, observe the spaces.

## 6. Swim

> Enter the water and swim.

Explain notes:

- Enter where you can.

## 7. Crowded

> No matter how crowded, you can swim there.

Explain notes:

- A crowded pool has spaces that keeps changing.

## 8. Shallow

> No matter how shallow, you can swim there.

## 9. Get out

> When to get out may depend on whether able to swim there or not.

## 10. Goal

> Swim without a goal.

Explain notes:

- Swimming how long doesn't really matter.
- Swimming how far doesn't really matter.

## 11. Fun

> Swimming should be fun.

## 12. Pool

> Swim in the pool as if you are walking in a street.

## 13. Beach

> Swim at the beach not affected by the waves.

Explain notes:

- Waves make it more fun.

## 14. Freestyle

> Choose freestyle.

## 15. Breathing

> Breathing on one side.

> One breath every four strokes.

## 16. Eyes

> Should look at the bottom of the water.

Explain notes:

- So your neck can be relaxed.

## 17. Rhythm

> Swim in a rhythm that suits you best.

## 18. Mistake

> Do not swim longer than needed.

> That will make the same mistakes longer.

## 19. Teacher

> Forget about what you learnt from a teacher.

## 20. Routine

> Wake up, brush teeth, go to swim.

## 21. Passion

> Swimming is no longer a passion.

## 22. Habit

> Swimming is not a habit.

## 23. Change

> Swimming is infinite change.

## 24. Ideas

> Swimming is source of ideas.

## 25. Wisdom

> Swimming is source of wisdom.

## 26. Feel

> Swimming is feeling what the water is like.

Explain notes:

- Feel what it is like and you will become water.

## 27. Balance

> Swimming is balance.

## 28. Hands

> Hands can pull back at any time.

> Hands can push water to the sides.

> The same hand can pull two times in a row.

## 29. Head

> Head can come out of the water and look to any direction steadily.

## 30. Legs

> Legs can stretch out to keep balance.

## 31. Knees

> Knees can touch the bottom of the pool.

## 32. Toes

> Toes can push the lower body up.

## 33. Body

> The body can tilt upwards.

> The body can bend up in the middle.

Explain notes:

- Both ways keep the head up, and you can see more above the water.

## 34. Wave

> A hand can also be used to wave above the water to greet a friend.

## 35. Movement

> Movement should be as soft as water.

## 36. Form

> How to swim doesn't really matter.

> Shallow or deep is really the same.

> Change the form accordingly.

## 37. Circles

> Try to swim in small circles at the beach.

## 38. Every pool

> Try to swim in every pool.

## 39. Lane

> In a swimming pool, you do not have to hold a lane.

Explain notes:

- You can swim left or right and find your lane within the spaces.
- It will eventually be your lane when you follow the same path, even if it is not a straight line.

## 40. Fast lane

> Avoid swimming in a fast lane if possible.

## 41. Guarding

> Guard with your upper arm touching theirs.

> Guard their legs.

> Guard your chest.

Explain notes:

- Palm can face upwards.

## 42. Space

> Better, place your hand there and leave some space.

## 43. Elsewhere

> Best swim elsewhere.

## 44. Compare

> Do not compare with others.

> Only compare with yourself.

## 45. Pass

> When a faster swimmer is passing you, let them pass.

## 46. Steady

> There is no swimming fast, but there is swimming steady.

Explain notes:

- Do not swim to exhaust your body.
- The best swimming is like having not been swimming.

## 47. Suit

> Wear a scuba diver suit and you can still swim at a good speed.

Explain notes:

- It becomes part of your body.
- It makes you like a balloon, so you float more at the beach, therefore safer.

## 48. Alert

> Better no need to stay alert than have to.

Explain notes:

- When you stay alert, you cannot enter the flow state.
- You need to stay alert because you are too close to another swimmer, or because you have not found your way in the water.

## 49. Reflection

> Think of a time you were calm in this pool and compare what it was like with now.

## 50. Master swimmer

> A master swimmer inspires others by showing them what they thought was not possible.
